id: I-001
claim: Quantizing Keys to 4 bits per token increases the number of denoising steps under confidence-threshold decoding (parallelism erosion), while quantizing Values to 4 bits does not.
status: supported (LLaDA2.1-mini, M1 and M2 Ultra, gen 128, temperature 0; with 4-, 8- and 16-bit weights) — the 2026-10-19 narrowing to 4-bit weights is WITHDRAWN
rests on: [F-001](../facts/F-001.md), [F-004](../facts/F-004.md); related accuracy-only asymmetry in S-001
falsifier: a pre-registered replication on at least a hundred prompts finds the paired per-prompt step increase for k4v8 not larger than for k8v4, or not different from zero.
alternatives considered: (a) n = 12 noise — step totals move by tens on single prompts; (b) a 4-bit-weight artefact rather than a KV effect (needs a BF16-weight control); (c) the effect is trajectory-specific (different text needs different step counts) rather than lower confidence.

update 2026-10-19: [F-005](../facts/F-005.md), [F-006](../facts/F-006.md) — per-token int4 Keys add steps (k4v8 +3.2/prompt, CI excludes 0) while int4 Values at int8 Keys do not (k8v4 CI spans 0). Effect size is small per prompt; the weights confound (BF16 control) is still open.

update 2026-10-19 (Studio batch): [F-010](../facts/F-010.md) replicates the effect on a second host with 4-bit weights (k4v8 +2.455, fp4 +3.170, CIs exclude 0; k8v4 CI spans 0). [F-011](../facts/F-011.md) — the same prompts with unquantized F16 weights on the same host — does not show it (k4v8 +0.509, fp4 +0.670, CIs span 0), so alternative (b) is open: erosion may be a 4-bit-weight interaction rather than a property of the model. Mechanism: [F-012](../facts/F-012.md) has fewer masked-position confidences above 0.7 in early block steps for k4v8 and fp4, but also for k8v4, so early-step deflation alone does not separate the eroding modes from the non-eroding one; [F-013](../facts/F-013.md) has first-divergence confidence at or above the baseline on fewer than half of divergent prompts in every mode.

correction 2026-10-19: the weight conclusion above rests on "significant in one artefact, not in the other". The direct per-prompt comparison of the two artefacts ([F-018](../facts/F-018.md), exploratory) has CIs spanning 0 for every mode (k4v8 +1.946 [−0.625, +4.571]) and is of similar size for k8v4 (+1.384), which does not erode. So the data say: Key erosion is detectable with 4-bit weights and not detectable with F16 weights; they do not establish that the two differ, nor that any weight effect is Key-specific. Quality side: [F-015](../facts/F-015.md) detects no GSM8K accuracy cost for any mode at n = 100 (largest net loss 2 prompts).

update 2026-10-19 (weight control, fresh prompts): the 4-bit narrowing above does not survive. On 200 unseen prompts k4v8 erodes with F16 weights (+2.450 [+1.230, +3.670]) and 8-bit weights (+1.850), while the 4-bit estimate (+1.340) spans 0 ([F-019](../facts/F-019.md)); the direct weight comparison is −1.110 [−3.040, +0.870] for k4v8, opposite in sign to a 4-bit-weight interaction ([F-020](../facts/F-020.md)). Pooled over 312 prompts the k4v8 effect is +1.740 / +1.833 / +1.753 for 4-/8-/16-bit weights ([F-022](../facts/F-022.md)). The earlier F16 null ([F-011](../facts/F-011.md)) is reproduced exactly on its own 112 prompts ([F-021](../facts/F-021.md)) — it is a property of that prompt set, not of the run. Best current reading: Key-quantization erosion of roughly two steps per 128-token generation, independent of weight precision within the resolution of these runs (200 fresh, 312 pooled prompts). Separately, 4-bit weights add steps with every cache mode (11210 vs 10421 / 10448 total with the uncompressed cache, [F-022](../facts/F-022.md)).
