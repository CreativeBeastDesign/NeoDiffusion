id: I-001
claim: Quantizing Keys to 4 bits per token increases the number of denoising steps under confidence-threshold decoding (parallelism erosion), while quantizing Values to 4 bits does not.
status: conjecture
rests on: [F-001](../facts/F-001.md), [F-004](../facts/F-004.md); related accuracy-only asymmetry in S-001
falsifier: a pre-registered replication on at least a hundred prompts finds the paired per-prompt step increase for k4v8 not larger than for k8v4, or not different from zero.
alternatives considered: (a) n = 12 noise — step totals move by tens on single prompts; (b) a 4-bit-weight artefact rather than a KV effect (needs a BF16-weight control); (c) the effect is trajectory-specific (different text needs different step counts) rather than lower confidence.
