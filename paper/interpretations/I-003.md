id: I-003
claim: KV-cache quantization at these context lengths does not make individual forwards faster on the dev M1; throughput differences between KV modes come from the number of forwards, not their cost.
status: supported (≤ 128-token prefixes on the M1; up to the 3307.5-token median prompts of F-027 on the M2 Ultra; 4-bit weights)
rests on: [F-002](../facts/F-002.md), [F-001](../facts/F-001.md)
falsifier: a prefix-length sweep shows ms/forward diverging between modes at longer contexts — which would bound, not refute, this claim.
alternatives considered: dequantization cost and bandwidth savings cancelling exactly — distinguishable only at longer prefixes.

update 2026-10-20 (context): with 3307.5 median-token prompts on the M2 Ultra, seconds per generation are 5.48 (uncompressed), 5.36 (k8v4), 5.35 (kivi4), 5.78 (k4v8) ([F-027](../facts/F-027.md)) — k8v4 and kivi4 are slightly faster per generation than the uncompressed cache (about 2 %), and the per-token int4-Key modes are slower through their extra steps.
