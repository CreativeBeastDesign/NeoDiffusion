id: I-004
claim: At long context, per-token 4-bit Key quantization is not a pure throughput cost: it also lowers task accuracy, while 4-bit Values (with 8-bit Keys) and per-channel 4-bit Keys do not detectably.
status: supported (narrow: LLaDA2.1-mini 4-bit weights, M2 Ultra, 10-shot GSM8K at 1725 median prompt tokens, gen 768, one run, n = 200)
rests on: [F-031](../facts/F-031.md), [F-033](../facts/F-033.md), [O-001](../observations/O-001.md); contrast with short-context [F-015](../facts/F-015.md), where no mode showed a detectable loss
falsifier: a pre-registered replication at the same context finds k4v8's paired accuracy difference CI including 0, or a second task at long context shows no k4v8 loss.
alternatives considered: (a) truncation — partly: 13 of the 32 losses are truncated generations, but the loss remains on untruncated pairs (F-033); (b) a GSM8K-specific effect — untested on other tasks; (c) the 4-bit weights compounding with 4-bit Keys — untested at long context (F-022 found no weight dependence of the step cost at short context).
